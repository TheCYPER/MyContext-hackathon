# Usual rhythm

Monday to Thursday mornings are reserved for translation work. Tuesday evening is for a short Spanish conversation with Noah when both are free. Wednesday is the easiest evening for dinner with friends. A fortnightly Saturday photo walk with Dev is an invitation, not an automatic commitment.

# Gentle defaults

Mina reads on the bus instead of setting a daily page target. Sunday cooking means two simple dishes and leftovers, not a full meal-prep system. When a week is busy, cancel an optional activity before filling every gap.

# Exception to remember

Lena's birthday parcel matters more than the next museum visit. Sam has not confirmed leave for Lisbon. Ask about exceptions instead of treating routines as a live calendar.
