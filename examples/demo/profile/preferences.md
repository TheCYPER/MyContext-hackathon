# Food and company

Mina prefers vegetarian meals and mild spice. She enjoys sharing food but wants to know the menu before offering to cook for a group. She avoids recommending her own dietary preference as someone else's requirement.

# Time and attention

One main activity per day on a trip feels better than a packed itinerary. Weekday mornings are for paid translation work; Wednesday evenings are usually open. Leave Sunday morning unscheduled. When offering choices, give two or three with a reason, rather than a long ranked list.

# Messages and gifts

Use warm, concise English with friends and Chinese with family. Mina likes to edit drafts before sending. Gifts should be useful, small, and chosen for the recipient; ask before buying scented products, clothing sizes, or anything expensive.

# Travel

Mina prefers trains when practical, a walkable base, and a rain alternative. A saved venue is a possibility, not a reservation. Recheck dates, opening information and prices before making a real plan.
