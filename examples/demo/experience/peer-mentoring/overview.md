# Role

Alex took part in a campus peer learning role from January through May 2026. The sessions centred on explaining course reasoning and helping peers identify the step where an argument stopped making sense. Maya Ortiz was a study collaborator; Theo Grant taught the relevant course work.

# What changed in his own learning

Preparing an explanation exposed a gap between following a derivation and reconstructing it. Alex could explain a chain-rule example with notes, then failed a new gradient exercise without them. A later successful example helped locate the remaining gap, rather than proving the topic was mastered.

StudyMap preserves those distinctions. It separates a recorded explanation, a guided attempt, and an independently solved exercise so a later assistant session can suggest an appropriate follow-up.

# Scope

The campus role ended in May. Alex still keeps the question library and studies with Maya, but he is not presenting an ongoing formal teaching appointment or a measured improvement in classmates' outcomes. The completed CourseQuery lab is a separate piece of course evidence.
