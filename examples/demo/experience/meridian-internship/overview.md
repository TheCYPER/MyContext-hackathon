# Role and period

Technical intern, June 16–August 22, 2025. Evan Cole supervised the work, and Jun Park was a collaborator on the engineering discussions. Alex contributed to local prototypes for animation transitions and asset review exports.

# Contributions

In Motion Atlas, Alex helped implement and inspect a blend baseline on 12 handpicked transition pairs. Three sharp-turn pairs showed visible foot sliding. The project did not establish held-out behavior or end-to-end latency, and the internship ended with a limited prototype.

In FrameBridge, Alex worked on an export that keeps reviewer comments and an asset version together. It provides review context; a reviewer still decides whether an asset is approved. There was no production integration during the internship.

# What the experience taught him

An apparently smooth demonstration can hide a narrow sample. Keeping a bad transition in the discussion made the scope easier to explain than a polished clip alone. In the review utility, preserving the human decision boundary was part of making the output useful.

# Current use

The internship is complete. Evidence Portfolio is preparing an account of Alex's contribution, and an unsent note asks Evan to check the wording. Do not turn the portfolio draft into a claim of measured speedup, sole ownership, or adoption by a production team.
