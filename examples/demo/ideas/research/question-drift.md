# Project Title

Can a dated question history explain why a paper entered or left a shortlist?

# Project Description

PaperTrail narrowed from robust evaluation to source-sequence leakage. Earlier paper choices become hard to interpret if notes silently adopt the latest wording of the question. Propose recording the question version beside each inclusion or exclusion reason.

Start with a small synthetic shortlist and one intentional scope change. Compare reconstructing the selection history from current notes alone with reconstructing it from dated scope decisions. Judge whether the explanation identifies the actual decision rule rather than retroactively inventing a reason that fits today's question.

This is a proposed study of traceability. It does not establish review completeness, discover a research gap, or prove that the papers were correctly interpreted. A well-preserved decision can still be a mistaken decision.

# What kind of help do you need from an advisor?

Ask Rhea to distinguish a useful refinement of scope from a change that invalidates the earlier search. Advice is needed on a small, assessable reconstruction task and on what evidence would support a claim of clearer decision history.
