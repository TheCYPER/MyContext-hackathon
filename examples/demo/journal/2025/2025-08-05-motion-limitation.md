# Observation

Three sharp-turn pairs in the 12-pair Motion Atlas inspection set show visible foot sliding. The blend executes, but the contact looks wrong. These are manual observations on a handpicked set, not a general failure rate or a benchmark against another method.

# Consequence

The internship account should describe a local transition prototype with unresolved cases. A smooth demonstration alone would hide the part of the task I learned the most from. I am keeping the sharp-turn examples in the handover notes instead of removing them from the sample.

# Open question

Before extending the method, I need a more representative evaluation and a clear way to assess contact behavior. The research idea asks how to design that evaluation; it is not an implemented fix. Held-out behavior and end-to-end latency remain unmeasured.
