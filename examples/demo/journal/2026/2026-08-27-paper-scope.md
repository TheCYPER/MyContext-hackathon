# Scope change

PaperTrail started with the broad phrase robust evaluation. That left me collecting interesting material without a clear reason to prefer one paper over another. The split problem gives the reading a concrete question: how source-sequence overlap can compromise the comparison I am trying to make.

# Working notes

For each shortlisted paper, I want the relevant claim, where it appears, and why it applies to this experiment. My interpretation should stay separate from the author's statement. A paper can be useful for a caveat without supplying a method I have implemented.

# Limit

This is a targeted shortlist, not a systematic review or a claim that the problem is novel. The advisor discussion should test the scope before I turn the notes into a wider research proposal.
