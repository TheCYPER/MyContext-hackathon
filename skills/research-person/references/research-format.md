# Person research format

## Files and IDs

- `people/<slug>/profile.md`: `id: person.<slug>`, `type: person`; concise current relationship and research router.
- `people/<slug>/research.md`: `id: person.<slug>.research`, `type: person`; dated source-backed research snapshot.

Use distinct stable IDs and the privacy level required by the selected context's schema and disclosure policy.

## Research record

Include the access date, identity-resolution evidence, verified current role, selected relevant work, and direct source links. For each selected work explain its contribution, date, evidence level, and why it affects this collaboration decision. Do not paste abstracts or long passages.

Describe concrete conversation questions and fit to the user's verified interests or projects. Label that fit as inference. Preserve unknowns such as availability, group scope, required background, and collaboration constraints instead of filling them in.

Research only relevant public professional information. Do not retain home addresses, relatives, protected traits, personal routines, private contact details, or personality speculation. Re-verify time-sensitive claims before external reuse.
