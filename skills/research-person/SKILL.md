# Research Person

Produce proportionate professional research that helps the user decide whether and how to engage with someone. Limit research to relevant public professional information; do not seek home addresses, private contact details, relatives, protected traits, or speculative personality judgments.

## Ground the task

Follow [context resolution and retrieval](../my-context/references/retrieval.md). Read the selected context's `AGENTS.md`, `INDEX.md`, and `profile/summary.md`, then only a relevant project or goal and any existing record about the person. Stay within five canonical documents in the first pass. Preserve prior relationship state; refresh only relevant or stale fields.

Resolve identity before combining sources. Keep similarly named people separate until affiliation or another primary identifier confirms a match.

## Research and explain fit

Use current primary sources for roles, affiliations, publications, and projects: official institutional pages, the person's public site or CV, authored papers, and artifact repositories. Record direct URLs and the date checked. Search-result snippets alone are not evidence.

Use evidence labels from the selected context's `meta/schema.md`. Separate verified facts about each party from inferred overlap. A broad job title does not establish availability, suitability, a specific research specialty, or agreement to collaborate.

Return useful source-grounded findings in the answer. Before preparing a persistent record, read [references/research-format.md](references/research-format.md) and the selected context's `meta/write-policy.md`. Keep `profile.md` a concise current router and `research.md` a dated research snapshot. Offer one consolidated durable update at task end when appropriate, or prepare it directly if the user explicitly requested storage. No personal-data mutation occurs before the policy's approval step.
